Two kinds of heat两种辣

麻

Má — the numbing

That fizzy, tingling buzz on the tongue isn't chilli — it's Sichuan peppercorn. Citrussy, floral, faintly anaesthetic. Locals call the sensation málà. First-timers think the dish is broken. Stay with it.

辣

Là — the spicy

Dried chillies, doubanjiang, sesame oil — building warmth, not punishment. Heat is graded on every menu page. If you want it dialled down, ask. If you want it dialled up, also ask.
