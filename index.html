About the kitchen

Real Sichuan, on Regent Street.

@72.China sits a few doors south of Downing Street, between Lensfield Road and the Catholic Church. We came to Cambridge with a single ambition — to cook Sichuan food the way it is cooked at home in Chengdu: numbing, fragrant, deeply layered, never timid. Alongside it we keep a careful Cantonese line — clean wok-fire, salt & pepper, sweet vinegar — for the dishes our guests have loved here for years.

“If you've never had real málà — the cool buzz of Sichuan peppercorn behind the chilli — let this be where you try it.”
